# Nemoviz HiFi Rack — privacy statement

NHR has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it sends nothing to its
author.

## What stays on your computer
Settings, caches, logs, presets, the tag backups and the music data NHR has
found are kept in your user folder (C:\Users\<name>\Nemoviz\NHR). You can delete
that folder at any time.

## What NHR sends, and to whom
Only when you have "Get metadata for your music" turned on, or use the feature
in question, NHR sends to public services the minimum needed to answer:
- MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Discogs, LRCLIB, lyrics.ovh, Deezer: artist,
  title and album names of the music you play, and disc IDs of audio CDs;
- AcoustID: an acoustic fingerprint of a track it could not identify otherwise;
- Radio Browser: the station names and filters you search for, and the station
  streams you listen to are fetched from their broadcasters;
- Spotify (only if you set up Spotify Connect with your own Client ID and sign
  in): the requests needed to show your library and control playback. The sign-in
  token is stored only on your computer.
Each service handles these requests under its own privacy policy. Your IP
address is visible to each service you contact, as with any internet request.
